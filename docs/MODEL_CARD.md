## 1. Intended Use, Scope & Users

- **Primary Purpose:** Provide Beninese agricultural producers, market traders (*collecteurs / grossistes / détaillants*), and food-security analysts with transparent `1`-, `2`-, and `3`-month ahead retail price forecasts (`XOF/kg`) and calibrated `80%` and `90%` prediction intervals across Benin's major staple food markets.
- **Forecasted Commodities (`Retail, KG, XOF, actual`):**
  1. **`Maize (white)` (*Maïs blanc*):** **`49` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `4,162` observed market-months).
  2. **`Tomatoes` (*Tomate fraîche*):** **`43` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `3,664` observed market-months).
  3. **`Yam (white)` (*Igname blanche / kokoro*):** **`19` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `1,603` observed market-months).
- **Out-of-Scope / Exploration-Only Series:**
  - Markets with $< 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019–2026` (`1` Tier-2 and `1` inactive market for `Maize (white)`; `6` Tier-2 and `1` Thin market for `Tomatoes`; `28` non-Tier-1 markets for `Yam (white)`) and generic/early `Yam` (*laboko*, `17` Tier-1 markets) are retained in the Explorer for historical inspection only, with an explicit UI notice explaining why no forecast is issued on sparse coverage.

## 2. Evaluation Protocol & Anti-Leakage Architecture

*Source script: [`src/evaluate/backtest.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/evaluate/backtest.py) → [`docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md)*

1. **Expanding-Window Rolling-Origin Split Geometry:**
   - **Initial Training Window (`36` months):** `2019-01-15` to `2021-12-15` (`3` complete annual cycles). First forecast origin is `2021-12-15` (index `35`).
   - **Validation Split (`2022-01-15..2025-09-15`):** All rolling-origin forecasts whose **target date $(t + h) \le \text{2025-09-15}$** (`45` target months for $h=1$, `44` for $h=2$, `43` for $h=3$; `6,300` observed evaluation pairs for `Maize (white)`, `5,502` for `Tomatoes`, `2,400` for `Yam (white)`). Used strictly for champion selection, parsimony thresholding, lag-imputation sensitivity, and weather ablation.
   - **Final Holdout Test Split (`2025-10-15..2026-09-15`):** All rolling-origin forecasts whose **target date $(t + h) \in [\text{2025-10-15}, \text{2026-09-15}]$** (`12` target months per horizon; `1,749` observed evaluation pairs for `Maize (white)`, `1,539` for `Tomatoes`, `678` for `Yam (white)`). Evaluated **once** after freezing champion model selection.
2. **Enforced Anti-Leakage Safeguards:**
   - **Safeguard 1 (No Leakage from Full-Sample Diagnostics):** `robust_z`, `is_outlier_level`, `is_outlier_jump`, and `is_outlier` are stripped in [`src/models/features.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/models/features.py) and never passed to any model.
   - **Safeguard 2 (Strict Ground-Truth Target Integrity):** Only market-months with `is_observed == 1` are ever used as training or evaluation targets (`0` imputed targets). The 1-month interpolated column `price_interp_1m_max` (`is_imputed_1m == 1`) is tested solely as a lag input in the validation sensitivity table ([`docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md)).

## 4. Honest Reporting: Where Complex Models & Weather Features Do Not Beat Baselines

1. **When Baselines Beat Complex Models (`Maize (white)` $h=1, 3$ and `Yam (white)` $h=3$):**
   - For **`Maize (white)` at $h=1\text{m}$**, storable grain prices follow a high-persistence local random walk from month to month: `Naive_LastValue` achieves a Validation MAE of **`19.77 XOF/kg` (`9.10%` MAPE)** vs `20.55 XOF/kg` (`9.47%` MAPE) for `HGBR_NoWeather` and `20.76 XOF/kg` (`9.48%` MAPE) for `Ridge_ARX` ([`docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md)). On the `2025-10..2026-09` Holdout Test, `Naive_LastValue` achieves **`9.56 XOF/kg` MAE (`6.77%` MAPE, `+85.0%` skill over `Seasonal_Naive_12m`'s `63.86 XOF/kg`)**.
   - At **$h=3\text{m}$** for both **`Maize (white)`** (`Val MAE = 35.05 XOF/kg` vs `Ridge_ARX` `35.26 XOF/kg`) and **`Yam (white)`** (`Val MAE = 73.18 XOF/kg` vs `Ridge_ARX` `73.46 XOF/kg`), the causal **`Seasonal_Ratio_Naive`** baseline—which anchors on the local market's trailing price level and steps forward by the market/zone detrended seasonal ratio—matches or slightly outperforms regularized regression and gradient boosting. Per `AGENTS.md`, we retain `Naive_LastValue` and `Seasonal_Ratio_Naive` in those 3 cells rather than forcing a more complex model.
2. **Why Pure `Seasonal_Naive_12m` ($y_{t+h-12}$) Fails During Regime Shifts:**
   - Pure 12-month lag lookup (`Seasonal_Naive_12m`) suffers from severe level lag whenever multi-year inflation or mean-reversion occurs: for `Maize (white)` on the `2025-10..2026-09` Holdout Test (after maize corrected downward from its `2022–2024` surge), `Seasonal_Naive_12m` has **`MAE = 63.86 XOF/kg` (`47.92%` MAPE)** and only **`50.8%` empirical 80% interval coverage**, whereas our selected champions achieve **`9.56 XOF/kg` ($h=1$), `21.32 XOF/kg` ($h=2$), and `24.57 XOF/kg` ($h=3$)** (`+61.5%` to `+85.0%` MAE skill improvement).
3. **Why Lagged NASA POWER Weather Features Do Not Beat Deterministic Seasonality (`docs/tables/phase2_weather_ablation.md`):**
   - Across all three products pooled over $h=1..3$ ([`docs/tables/phase2_weather_ablation.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_weather_ablation.md)), adding the 9 lagged NASA POWER agroclimatic features (`HGBR_WithWeather` vs `HGBR_NoWeather`) yields **`-0.25%` skill change on Validation (`28.77` vs `28.70 XOF/kg`) and `-1.20%` on Holdout Test (`21.89` vs `21.63 XOF/kg`) for `Maize (white)`**, **`+0.03%` on Validation (`137.09` vs `137.13 XOF/kg`) and `-0.68%` on Holdout Test (`135.62` vs `134.71 XOF/kg`) for `Tomatoes`**, and **`-0.20%` on Validation (`59.33` vs `59.21 XOF/kg`) and `-1.73%` on Holdout Test (`56.04` vs `55.08 XOF/kg`) for `Yam (white)`**.
   - **Agronomic & Statistical Explanation:** As anticipated in Phase 1(b) EDA, while raw lagged rainfall (`precip_lag3m`) and temperature (`t2m_lag1m`) correlate with the seasonal cycle, deterministic calendar features (`month` Fourier harmonics + causal market/zone seasonal indices) already capture that regular annual rhythm with zero estimation variance, while deseasonalized rainfall anomalies (`precip_anom_z_lag1m`) explain $< 1\%$ of monthly price variance in `7.75` years of data. Under the `>= 0.5%` parsimony rule, `HGBR_WithWeather` is excluded from all deployed champions.
4. **Lag-Imputation Sensitivity (`docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md`):**
   - Using isolated 1-month linear interpolation (`price_interp_1m_max`) strictly to fill missing historical lags (while keeping `0` imputed forecast targets per Safeguard 2) yields a small validation MAE reduction of **`-0.19 XOF/kg` (`+0.65%`) for `Ridge_ARX` on `Maize (white)`**, **`-2.23 XOF/kg` (`+1.66%`) for `Ridge_ARX` on `Tomatoes`**, and **`-0.64 XOF/kg` (`+1.08%`) for `HGBR_NoWeather` on `Yam (white)`** ([`docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md)). However, our primary deployed models use strictly raw observed lags (`use_interp_lags=False`) so that zero synthetic values enter either features or targets in production.
5. **Outlier Evaluation Sensitivity (`docs/tables/phase2_outlier_sensitivity.md`):**
   - All primary metrics in Section 3 evaluate on **100% of observed ground-truth target months (`is_observed == 1`, including flagged spikes/jumps)**. Excluding target months flagged with `is_outlier == 1` ([`docs/tables/phase2_outlier_sensitivity.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_outlier_sensitivity.md)) lowers pooled Holdout Test MAE (`h=1..3`) by **`-0.15 XOF/kg` (`18.48` → `18.33 XOF/kg`, MAPE `13.48%` → `13.29%`) for `Maize (white)`**, **`-2.08 XOF/kg` (`139.01` → `136.93 XOF/kg`, MAPE `24.65%` → `24.35%`) for `Tomatoes`** (and `-9.06 XOF/kg` on Validation where `329/5,502` target pairs were jump/level outliers), and **`-4.50 XOF/kg` (`56.99` → `52.50 XOF/kg`, MAPE `14.28%` → `13.08%`, `80%` PI coverage rising from `76.4%` to `79.6%`) for `Yam (white)`**.

2. **Known Operational Limits & Where the Models Are Less Reliable:**
   - **High-Volatility Perishable Tomato Markets (`Tomatoes` in Southern/Plateau Deficit Markets):** While `Ridge_ARX` achieves **`9.8%` Holdout Test MAPE in `Parakou` (`MAE = 45.8 XOF/kg`)**, **`10.7%` in `Savalou` (`66.4 XOF/kg`)**, **`14.3%` in `Bassila` (`41.2 XOF/kg`)**, and **`14.5%` in `Bohicon` (`66.9 XOF/kg`)**, error rises substantially in high-dispersion Southern markets such as **`Tatonnoukon` (`Test Mean = 3,525.4 XOF/kg`, `MAE = 1,447.0 XOF/kg`, `MAPE = 39.6%`)**, **`Kétou` (`MAPE = 46.7%`)**, and **`Zè` (`MAPE = 127.1%` during a local price collapse to `171.9 XOF/kg`)** ([`docs/tables/phase2_metrics_by_market.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_metrics_by_market.md)).
   - **Unpredictable Sudden External Shocks:** Statistical and ML models conditioned on historical prices and lagged climate cannot anticipate sudden trade border closures, fuel subsidy removals, or localized transport strikes before they appear in market price data.
   - **3-Month Horizon Harvest Transitions for `Yam (white)`:** On the Holdout Test, `Yam (white)` accuracy is strong during the **`Lean / High-Price Season` (`MAPE = 10.71%`, `MAE = 50.72 XOF/kg`, `80% PI Coverage = 82.2%`)** and in the **`South (<7.5°N)` (`MAPE = 12.10%`, `MAE = 51.01 XOF/kg`)**, but error widens during the **`Harvest / Low-Price Season` (`MAPE = 18.00%`, `MAE = 68.55 XOF/kg`, `80% PI Coverage = 66.7%`)** when the exact week of new-tuber arrival shifts between August and October ([`docs/tables/phase2_metrics_by_zone_season.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_metrics_by_zone_season.md)).
