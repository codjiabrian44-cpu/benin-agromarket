## 1. Intended Use, Scope & Users

- **Primary Purpose:** Provide Beninese agricultural producers, market traders (*collecteurs / grossistes / détaillants*), and food-security analysts with transparent `1`-, `2`-, and `3`-month ahead retail price forecasts (`XOF/kg`), calibrated `80%` and `90%` prediction intervals, and holdout-derived reliability badges across Benin's major staple food markets.
- **Forecasted Commodities (`Retail, KG, XOF, actual`):**
  1. **`Maize (white)` (*Maïs blanc*):** **`49` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `4,162` observed market-months).
  2. **`Tomatoes` (*Tomate fraîche*):** **`43` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `3,664` observed market-months).
  3. **`Yam (white)` (*Igname blanche / kokoro*):** **`19` Tier-1 markets** ($\ge 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019-01..2026-09`, `1,603` observed market-months).
- **Out-of-Scope / Exploration-Only Series:**
  - Markets with $< 80 / 93$ observed months in `2019–2026` (`1` Tier-2 and `1` `None` [0 observations] market for `Maize (white)`; `6` Tier-2, `1` Thin, and `1` `None` market for `Tomatoes`; `21` Tier-2, `7` Thin, and `4` `None` markets for `Yam (white)`) and generic/early `Yam` (*laboko*, `17` Tier-1 markets) are retained in the Explorer for historical inspection only, with an explicit UI notice explaining why no forecast is issued on sparse coverage ([`docs/tables/coverage_by_market.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/coverage_by_market.md), verified in [`docs/tables/phase2_export_validation.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_export_validation.md)).

## 2. Evaluation Protocol, Anti-Leakage Architecture & Protocol Integrity (`M1`)

*Source scripts: [`src/evaluate/backtest.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/evaluate/backtest.py) → [`docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_backtest_by_product_horizon.md); [`src/evaluate/recompute_audit.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/evaluate/recompute_audit.py) → [`docs/tables/phase2_recompute_diff.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_recompute_diff.md)*

1. **Expanding-Window Rolling-Origin Split Geometry:**
   - **Initial Training Window (`36` months):** `2019-01-15` to `2021-12-15` (`3` complete annual cycles). First forecast origin is `2021-12-15` (index `35`).
   - **Validation Split (`2022-01-15..2025-09-15`):** All rolling-origin forecasts whose **target date $(t + h) \le \text{2025-09-15}$** (`45` target months for $h=1$, `44` for $h=2$, `43` for $h=3$; `6,300` observed evaluation pairs for `Maize (white)`, `5,502` for `Tomatoes`, `2,400` for `Yam (white)`). Used strictly for champion selection, parsimony thresholding (`>= 0.5%` Validation MAE reduction required over the best baseline), lag-imputation sensitivity, and weather ablation.
   - **Final Holdout Test Split (`2025-10-15..2026-09-15`):** All rolling-origin forecasts whose **target date $(t + h) \in [\text{2025-10-15}, \text{2026-09-15}]$** (`12` target months per horizon; `1,749` observed evaluation pairs for `Maize (white)`, `1,539` for `Tomatoes`, `678` for `Yam (white)`). Evaluated **once** after freezing champion model selection.
2. **Enforced Anti-Leakage Safeguards:**
   - **Safeguard 1 (No Leakage from Full-Sample Diagnostics):** `robust_z`, `is_outlier_level`, `is_outlier_jump`, and `is_outlier` are stripped in [`src/models/features.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/models/features.py) and never passed to any model.
   - **Safeguard 2 (Strict Ground-Truth Target Integrity):** Only market-months with `is_observed == 1` are ever used as training or evaluation targets (`0` imputed targets). The 1-month interpolated column `price_interp_1m_max` (`is_imputed_1m == 1`) is tested solely as a lag input in the validation sensitivity table ([`docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_imputation_sensitivity.md)).
3. **Protocol Governance Limitation (`M1`) & Recompute Audit Verification:**
   - **Protocol Commit Limitation (`M1`):** Although the Phase 2 evaluation protocol (`Decision 7` in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/DECISIONS.md)) was written before running the backtest, it was committed in the same git commit (`a80ef75`) as the Phase 2 modeling outputs rather than in its own standalone pre-evaluation commit as prescribed by `AGENTS.md` (`## Protocol integrity`).
   - **Cryptographic Cache Provenance & Bit-for-Bit Recompute Audit:** To ensure strict reproducibility, cached predictions (`data/processed/phase2_backtest_predictions.csv`, `data/processed/phase2_future_forecasts_all_models.csv`) and model artifacts (`models/champion_*.joblib`) store sidecar `.sha256.json` files recording the SHA-256 digest of all source modules (`src/config.py`, `src/models/features.py`, `src/models/forecasters.py`, `src/evaluate/backtest.py`) and input datasets. Re-running `python -m src.evaluate.backtest --recompute` from scratch against the archived pre-recompute artifacts (`docs/audit/before_recompute/`, [`docs/audit/README.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/audit/README.md)) produced the **exact same SHA-256 digest (`2a474fff86f1d2fa45243149985884e0a49db99aa93c1e4c2a3c86a6516bd87b` across all `181,680` backtest rows)**, with `0 / 9` selected champions changed and `0.000000 XOF/kg` maximum difference across all `144` `(product, split, horizon, model)` slices ([`docs/tables/phase2_recompute_diff.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_recompute_diff.md)).

### 3.1 Champion Selection & Headline Accuracy Against the Best Baseline (`docs/tables/phase2_champion_selection.md`)

*Source script: [`src/evaluate/backtest.py`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/src/evaluate/backtest.py) → [`docs/tables/phase2_champion_selection.md`](file:///home/brian/projets-ds/benin-agromarket/docs/tables/phase2_champion_selection.md)*

- **Why Headline Accuracy Must Be Stated Against the Best Baseline (Not `Seasonal_Naive_12m`):**
  Pure 12-month lag lookup (`Seasonal_Naive_12m`, $\hat{y}_{t+h|t} = y_{t+h-12}$) is a weak reference whenever multi-year inflation or mean reversion shifts the price level between year $t-1$ and year $t$ (for example, on `Maize (white)` during the `2025-10..2026-09` Holdout Test, `Seasonal_Naive_12m` has `MAE = 63.86 XOF/kg` and `47.92%` MAPE because it projects `2024–2025` higher prices onto the `2025–2026` post-correction regime). By contrast, **`Naive_LastValue`** ($\hat{y}_{t+h|t} = y_t$) and **`Seasonal_Ratio_Naive`** (which anchors on the local market's current price level and steps forward by the causal market/zone detrended seasonal index ratio) adapt immediately to local level shifts and represent strong, realistic operational baselines.
- **Headline Holdout Test Accuracy vs. the Best Baseline (`2025-10..2026-09`):**
  1. **`Maize (white)` (`49` Tier-1 markets):**
     - **$h=1\text{m}$:** The best baseline **`Naive_LastValue` is itself the selected champion** (`Val MAE = 19.8 XOF/kg`, **`Test MAE = 9.6 XOF/kg`**, `Test RMSE = 15.2 XOF/kg`, **`Test MAPE = 6.8%`**, `0.0%` skill vs best baseline, `+85.0%` vs `Seasonal_Naive_12m`), as no statistical or ML model beat last-value persistence on validation (`HGBR_NoWeather` `Val MAE = 20.5 XOF/kg`, `-3.9%`).

### 3.2 Holdout Rank Comparison: Cases Where the Selected Model Did Worse on the Holdout Than Another Candidate (`docs/tables/phase2_holdout_rank_comparison.md`)

### 5.3 High-Volatility Perishable Markets & External Shock Limits
- **High-Volatility Southern Tomato Markets (`docs/tables/phase2_metrics_by_market.md`):** While `Ridge_ARX` achieves **`9.8%` Holdout Test MAPE in `Parakou` (`MAE = 45.8 XOF/kg`)**, **`10.7%` in `Savalou` (`66.4 XOF/kg`)**, **`14.3%` in `Bassila` (`41.2 XOF/kg`)**, and **`14.5%` in `Bohicon` (`66.9 XOF/kg`)**, error rises substantially in high-dispersion Southern markets such as **`Tatonnoukon` (`Test Mean = 3,525.4 XOF/kg`, `MAE = 1,447.0 XOF/kg`, `MAPE = 39.6%`)**, **`Kétou` (`MAPE = 46.7%`)**, and **`Zè` (`MAPE = 127.1%` during a local price collapse to `171.9 XOF/kg`)**.
- **Unpredictable Sudden External Shocks:** Statistical and ML models conditioned on historical prices and lagged climate cannot anticipate sudden trade border closures, fuel subsidy removals, or localized transport strikes before they appear in market price data.
